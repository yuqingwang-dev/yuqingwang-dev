## Customization

The original twelve-column grid, baseline positions, rules and typography hierarchy are retained. The supplied placeholders are replaced with Sunny's real project information. Name sizing is adjusted for GenRecAgent and Qingtu. A restrained blue accent, tinted project backgrounds and dark variants are applied.

The three assets are adaptations of the same downloaded SVG. They are stored locally in this repository, with no third-party statistics widget, tracking pixel, scheduled workflow or external font dependency.

Editable assets: `assets/swiss-grid/sunny.svg`, `genrecagent.svg`, `qingtu.svg` and their `-dark.svg` variants. Visible descriptions and project links are in `README.md`.
